Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

BUYBUDDY is a personal shopping and delivery marketplace for Nigeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the BUYBUDDY Android app and to buybuddy.ng and its current hosted address.

1. Who we are

BUYBUDDY ("we", "us") operates the marketplace that connects customers, personal shoppers, riders and stores. For any question about this policy or your data, contact privacy@buybuddy.ng.

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission's guidance (NDPR).

2. What we collect

CategoryWhat it isWhy we need it
Account Name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), role To create and secure your account and let you sign in
Delivery Saved addresses, delivery notes, the phone number your rider calls To deliver your order to the right place
Orders Items bought, amounts, payment status, order history, receipts, in-app messages To fulfil orders, support you afterwards and meet accounting rules

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.
  • We do not store your full card details.
  • We do not read your in-app messages except where a support or safety issue requires it.
  • We do not turn on location tracking for shoppers or riders when they are not on an active job.

4. Who we share it with

  • Stores — the items and quantities in your order, so they can prepare it.
  • Your shopper and rider — your name, delivery address and phone number while the order is active.
  • Payment processor (Paystack or Flutterwave) — to take payment and pay out to stores and workers.
  • Messaging provider — your phone number, to send one-time codes and order updates.
  • Hosting and analytics providers — strictly to run and secure the service.
  • Authorities — only where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept for as long as your account is open and then up to 6 years afterwards, because Nigerian tax and accounting rules require it. Photos you send for "Shop for me" are kept with the order so you have a record of what was bought. You can ask us to delete anything that is not legally required to be kept.

6. Your rights

  • See what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — for example, turn off marketing notifications or location sharing.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if we get it wrong.

To use any of these, email privacy@buybuddy.ng from the address on your account. We reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it, and every administrator action is logged. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the Commission within the time the law allows.

8. Children

BUYBUDDY is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
